# Welcome, grown-up

This is a friend-style manual for adults who game on a PC — whether you are
returning after years away, starting fresh because your kids (or students,
or spouse) play, or simply finally reading a manual because you are tired
of guessing. It explains things the way a friend would: plain English,
step by step, never condescending, and with a warm bias toward
**Final Fantasy XIV** (since 2026 it is pronounced "the game we obsess
over") — but every chapter is written so it helps you with any PC game.

We write it in the same spirit as [The Massive AI Manual](https://dylanasdale.github.io):
no gatekeeping, no jargon deployed to exclude, no "just Google it."

## What this manual promises

- **You will not feel dumb.** Every term gets defined, and the
  [glossary](glossary.md) collects them all in one warm pile.
- **You will not lose progress.** Chapter 4 makes sure of it —
  [save-file backups](ch-04-save-backups.md) so nothing is ever lost.
- **You will not overspend.** Chapter 2 ([modest hardware](ch-02-hardware.md))
  is about buying sensibly, not buying impressively.
- **You will not break anything.** Chapter 6 ([mod safety](ch-06-mod-safety.md))
  is written in plain rules you can follow in one sitting.
- **You will stay comfortable.** Chapters 3 and 7 treat your body
  (hands, eyes, back) as serious gear — because they are.

## One promise we make to you

**Comfort is the whole strategy.** Games are better when you are rested,
backed up, comfortable, and not broke. A grown-up gamer is not a person
with reflexes like lightning; a grown-up gamer is someone who has a save
they can restore, a budget they did not feel guilt about, a chair their
back forgives them for, and friends they can be honest with
("it's 10 p.m.; I'm logging off, my sweet prince").

> If you take one thing from this manual: **back up your saves first,
> buy hardware second, and only then care about anything flashy.**
